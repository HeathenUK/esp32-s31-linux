# Performance plan, 2026-09-23

The order of work drawn from `perf-review-2026-09-23.md` (183 proposals, three
lenses each), after a second review of that report's own recommendations.
Read the review for the evidence; this file is the plan and its running log.

## What the review established

- No remaining lever is of SMP / RAM-text / X11-flattening magnitude. Of 95
  reviewed candidates, zero rated large; of 88 discovered, one rated medium
  (the CPU PLL overclock, weak on its other two lenses).
- What remains is 1-4% items, most individually smaller than the per-boot
  lottery (+-10% on Quake, ~17% on the desktop). They cannot be validated one
  at a time on fps.
- Quake at 320x240 with sound lands at **17.5-18.2 fps** with everything
  shipped, **18.2-18.7** at 360 MHz, **18.8-19.7** only at a stable 400 MHz.
  20 fps is not reliably reachable without the overclock or a rule change.

## Where this plan departs from the report's order

1. **The per-boot lottery is the largest known effect** (10-17%) and is what
   makes every other item unmeasurable. The cache counters (T1.5) are the
   instrument that the earlier chase never had. Point them at a slow boot
   before anything else; if nothing shows, close it and design around it.
2. **C45 (the 7-8 ms kworker hand-off) is Tier 1, not a footnote.** That
   constant forbids moving lvdesk's 3.6 ms expand-and-present per frame off
   CPU0 onto the 64%-idle CPU1: ~6% of CPU0 at 16 fps, larger than C39. The
   CLIC-level bug that plausibly caused it was fixed (patches/0057). One
   same-boot A/B/A with existing 0644 params, no build.
3. **The 240 MHz mirror arm is information, not a step toward shipping 400.**
   400 MHz runs the shared cache domain 25% over spec; the failure mode is
   silent PSRAM corruption. 360 is the realistic ceiling.
4. **T1.9 (storage) is demoted.** `MMC_CAP_DONE_COMPLETE` is set by no
   in-tree driver and silently disables CMD13 write-error detection; C60 is a
   week for +1.2%. Run the free SD-stat census; otherwise leave storage alone.

## Rules that hold throughout

- Nothing below the lottery ships on its own fps number. Bundle, and judge on
  pingpong medians, `/proc/interrupts` rates and h1s sample shares; fps only as
  a multi-boot median at the end.
- Fresh boot per arm where the effect is claimed on fps; same-boot A/B/A only
  with the A-to-A gap reported as the error bar (dead-end #19).
- Nothing touches the board while a measurement is in flight. Every board
  test <= 10 minutes per result.
- App code, app placement (SD), Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are untouchable.

## Phase 0 - bank the free wins (one build, no board risk)

- [x] **C37** `--disable FRAME_POINTER` for non-PROF/DIAG builds. One
      `make linux`; confirm `.config`, read the partition-free line. Expect
      slack 119,803 -> ~300 kB and 1.5-2.5% of CPU0 (resolvable on pingpong,
      never fps). Keep it on for `PROF=1` / `DIAG=1`.
- [x] **C18** fix `vmlinux-xip.lds.S` so the section list binds to every
      glob, not only the last (`__of_table_esp32s31_systimer` is inside
      `.text.fast`, 22.6 kB of never-executed text rides along), and the
      early-NX mapping that stops RAM text executing before `setup_vm_final`.
      Keep the resurrected systimer clocksource disabled or lower-rated. Boot
      check with alive.py; the last bad linker edit produced #334.
- [x] **X2-14** user-PC symbolisation in `scripts/board/h1s-report.py`
      (port from `rootfs/pcsample.c`), against the `.dbg` twins in `rootfs/`.
      Retro-fits every capture in `artifacts/`.

## Phase 1 - instruments and cheap gates (each <= 10 min, no build, one at a time)

- [ ] **T1.5** L1 cache counters at 0x2C000180 via devmem. Record register
      state first; `reset.py` ready (a neighbouring register killed the hart
      twice, memory `s31-icache-autoload-wedges`). Validate on pingpong cold
      vs hot. D-side (DBUS0/1) is the new number; I-side is contaminated.
- [ ] **Lottery**: 3-4 fresh boots, pingpong + counters on each. A slow boot
      with a distinct refill/D-side signature is a cause to chase; none means
      close it for good.
- [ ] **C45** re-price the kworker hand-off: `dirtybench` A/B/A over
      `defer_copy` / `ppa_async` / `ppa_spin_us`. If it is ~1 ms now,
      moving expand-and-present to CPU1 becomes the SMP project.
- [ ] **T2.3** `vma_ra_enabled=0` + page-cluster 2: two fresh-boot timedemos,
      judged on majflt and `/sys/block/mmcblk0/stat` fields 1 and 3.
- [ ] **SD census** (rides on the above): sectors / majflt sizes the storage
      question and probably closes it.
- [ ] **XLITE_NOSYNCWAIT** 5-line bypass in `xlite/xlite.c` XSync, on the
      sdl1 canary: bounds the whole "last X round trip" family in one boot.

## Phase 2 - the 240 MHz mirror arm (~1 hour, one build+flash chain)

- [ ] Both timebases moved (`esp32s31.dtsi:196` and `S31_TIMEBASE_HZ`),
      `make linux` AND `make opensbi`, the frequency in `bootloader/sdkconfig`
      (not sdkconfig.defaults), flash all three. Validate against the
      loader's `cpu freq:` line and a host stopwatch on the demo; an
      unvalidated arm returns a false null (fps inflated by 320/240).
      Shallow slope: drop the overclock and discount the text family.
      Steep slope: 360 MHz is promoted to Phase 5.

## Phase 3 - the text family as one bundle, judged by mechanism

- [ ] C20 + C19 (interrupt/entry spine; blocked objects left out), then
- [ ] C23 prelock sweep 2/4/8 kB (A/B/A, A-to-A gap as error bar), then
- [ ] C39 function-granular, profile-ordered text, aimed by the counters.
      Joint cap +4-6% of CPU0. Do not expect the sum.

## Phase 4 - disjoint items, in value order

- [ ] **C86** libc math, gated on the LD_PRELOAD caller census (40 lines).
- [ ] **C32** 4 MB megapages, gated on the stride probe; SECRETMEM off in the
      same build.
- [ ] **T2.1** hygiene bundle, without the excluded arms (PPA/GDMA masking,
      the entropy arm, `noirqdebug` as a default).
- [ ] **T2.2** touch poll scoped to fullscreen.

## Phase 5 - only if Phase 2 was steep

- [ ] 360 MHz with a multi-boot soak. 400 only with a corruption check over
      PSRAM under load, and only if 360 delivers.
