5. NOT DONE, AND WHY (ranked by what is left)
  - The fillers' triangle setup and span-plane evaluation (~1.7 M/frame
    TEXFILTER=0, section 1) is now the largest geometry-side cost. Its
    instructions are mostly stack traffic: a 64-instruction span iteration
    re-evaluates every plane from the reference pixel (bit-exactness
    forbids stepping them incrementally) and stores ~30 ZSpan fields per
    triangle. It is in ztriangle_gen.c, where the concurrent second-
    rasteriser-thread work lives, so it was left alone: the lever is to
    store the plane coefficients once per triangle in the ZSpan and let
    zp_run evaluate them at the span start (one call's arguments instead
    of per-span stores), measured there.
  - to_vg: 58 instructions a triangle vertex, 5,247 calls for ~4,650
    vertices. A per-vertex cache of the filler vertex saves only the
    ~12% re-conversions (~50 k/frame) against invalidation in four places
    (polygon offset, two-sided swap, clipped vertices, colour-only
    updates): not worth its risk.
  - The immediate-mode entry chain (glVertex3fv -> tgl_glVertex4f ->
    vtx_run -> gl_vertex4f checks -> core): 18 instructions a vertex, of
    which ~5 are removable by pointing vtx_run at a discard stub outside
    glBegin; it interacts with the RAM-text lever (vtx_run is its hook).
  - tu_swap (426 a call, 102/frame) is state code, not geometry.
  - The vertex core itself (150 a vertex, no lighting): transform, clip
    code, the colour and texcoord copies and primitive assembly; nothing
    left in it that is not needed for bit-identical output.
