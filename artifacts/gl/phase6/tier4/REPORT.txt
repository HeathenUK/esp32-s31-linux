SUMMARY
  The cache is indexed by the PHYSICAL address and a way is 32 kB, so set
  bits 12-14 come from the page frames Linux hands out; only the offset
  inside a 4 kB page is the library's to choose. Tier 3's "12% from heap
  placement" was measured with physical = virtual (bare-metal qemu) and
  does not carry to the board as such. With the board's placement
  modelled (random frames for every heap / stack / data page, colour
  buffers contiguous as the zero-copy CMA pixmaps are):
  - Shipped, S31GL_CCOLOR=1 (default): the GLX layer puts the depth
    buffer at the in-page offset farthest from every colour buffer
    (2 kB for page-aligned SHM / zero-copy segments; today it sits a chunk
    header (16 B, musl's mmap'd calloc - not checked on the board) into
    its page, the same in-page line as the colour pixel).
      PSRAM traffic, 26 random placements: 2,905.5 -> 2,848.7 kB/frame
      mean (-1.94%), better in 26 of 26 (-0.7% .. -3.4%).
      physical = virtual model, the 8 page-granular depth positions:
      mean 2,713.9 -> 2,669.7 (-1.6%), worst 2,937 -> 2,726 (-7.2%).
    0 instructions in the core (the pixels and every hash are the same by
    construction; the placement is decided once per drawable allocation),
    4 kB of address space and at most one resident page per drawable.
  - Found, NOT implementable by address choice: the per-launch lottery is
    the hot small pages - the rasteriser's stack page, the GLContext's
    three pages, the GOT / .data page, two .rodata table pages - landing
    on the same page colour (frame bits 12-14) with overlapping hot
    in-page lines. Across 26 placements traffic is 2,708 .. 4,095 kB a
    frame (sd 272, +51% at the worst); an oracle arm that only forces
    those 8 pages onto distinct colours reads 2,652 .. 2,830 (sd 41,
    mean -3.7%, the worst -33%). Userspace cannot pick its frames by
    virtual address; section 3 has what could.

1. THE INSTRUMENT (gl/bench/qemu/cachesim.c, cachesim.sh, ccsweep.sh)
  cachesim.c gained a board placement mode:
    pages=1,seed=S[,col=ADDR:LEN ...][,fix=VPAGE:C ...][,pagestat=1|2|3]
  Every 4 kB virtual page gets a random physical frame on first touch
  (without replacement, a 128 MB pool shuffled by the seed); each col=
  range stays physically contiguous from a random page of its own (the
  zero-copy colour buffers are dumb buffers from the lcd_reserved CMA
  pool - lvdesk/xshim.c win8_gem_alloc; MIT-SHM segments would be paged,
  pages=1 without col=). fix= forces a page's colour (the oracle arm);
  pagestat= prints the pages (and lines) with the most refills, or with
  =3 the most accesses. Without pages= it is tier 3's model, unchanged
  (the reference run on this image: 2,879.5 kB/frame).
  q_replay.c gained placement knobs for the model (qr.env, via QSR_ENV):
    QR_CALIGN=1  each colour buffer on its own page (as SHM / zero-copy)
    QR_DZ=N      depth at (Z - fb0) mod 32768 = N
    QR_MAP=1     prints the drawables' buffers and every block >= 256 B
  ccsweep.sh OUT LIST [JOBS] runs a list of arms in parallel (~10 s an
  arm, 10 at a time). A first attempt that streamed every access to a
  file for offline replay was dropped: 2.85 M line-runs a frame, 0.9 GB.
  Geometry of the replay: 320x240, fb0 0x83f1f000, fb1 0x83f46000 (39
  pages apart: (C1 - C0) mod 32K = 28,672), depth 153,620 B.

       mean 2,905.5 -> 2,848.7 (-1.94%), median -1.83%, 26 of 26 better.
     Why: with the same in-page offset a colour line and the depth line of
     the same pixels share a set whenever the two frames agree in bits
     12-14 - one page pair in eight - and any third line (texel, table,
     stack) then evicts one of them. 2 kB apart they are never in one set.
  The rule shipped (gl/glx/glx_present.c depth_alloc, S31GL_CCOLOR=0|1,
  default 1): calloc 4 kB more than the depth buffer needs and start it at
  the 16 B step, within that 4 kB, whose in-page distance to the nearest
  colour buffer of the drawable (1 MIT-SHM segment, 2, or 2-3 zero-copy
  pixmaps, or a plain malloc'd buffer) is largest; computed from the
  addresses shmat / the zero-copy mmap / malloc actually returned, once
  per allocation. For page-aligned segments and a 16 B-aligned calloc the
  distance is 2,032 .. 2,048 B, whatever the header's size. S31GL_CCOLOR=0 is the old calloc exactly.
  The replay's QR_DZ=2048 is the same placement (a 16 B difference inside
  a line). RAM: +4,096 B of address space per drawable; calloc of a
  mmapped chunk touches nothing, and the buffer's pages are touched as it
  is, so resident cost is at most one page (the span grows by < 4 kB).
  Instructions: none in the core; GLX adds a 256 x nbuf-step loop at
  allocation (~5 k instructions once per drawable).

3. THE LOTTERY: HOT SMALL PAGES (model/hotpages-*, hotlines-s3.txt,
   hot-accesses.txt, pages-oracle.txt)
  pagestat=3 (accesses, physical = virtual): the stack's top page
  (0x8ffff, 1.81 M accesses a frame over ~22 lines, 0x640-0xe00 in the
  page), the GLContext block and its companions (0x83f16/17/18, 0.93 M),
  two texture pages (0.26 M, 0.19 M on one line), .rodata tables
  (0x80055/56, 81 k) and the GOT (.data 0x80058, 20 k) and .bss (0x8007f,
  16 k). On the board each of those is an arbitrary frame; P(at least two
  of 8 pages on one of 8 colours) = 1 - 8!/8^8 ~ 99.8%, and it costs
  when their hot in-page lines overlap: seed 3 has the stack, the
  context's first page, a texture page and the GOT page all on colour 6,
  their hot lines all at 0xa80-0xe00 - 4,095 kB a frame, +51% over the
  floor. Per placement (dz=2048 throughout):
     random  2,855 2,798 4,095 2,754 2,786 2,708 2,710 2,751 3,227 2,811
             2,866 2,739 2,731 2,710 2,712 2,860 2,737 2,757 2,725 2,872
             3,005 2,733 2,747 2,862 2,789 2,726    mean 2,848.7 sd 272.5
     oracle  2,692 2,764 2,736 2,724 2,751 2,697 2,727 2,707 2,830 2,652
             2,710 2,705 2,807 2,716 2,727 2,719 2,703 2,766 2,734 2,792
             2,741 2,770 2,791 2,801 2,782 2,772    mean 2,742.8 sd 41.4
  (oracle: fix= the stack, the three context pages, .data, the two
  .rodata pages and .bss onto colours 0-7.) That is a -3.7% mean and the
  whole tail - a candidate mechanism for memory note
  s31-canary-per-boot-bimodal (~17% between boots, ~1% within one) - but
  not a lever the library's address choices reach: the context's hot
  lines already span almost every in-page offset across its three pages,
  the stack's depth is the application's, and the GOT's offset is fixed
  by the link. What could, not attempted (time box):
    - pick frames: allocate a few spare pages, read their PFNs from
      /proc/self/pagemap (root; needs CONFIG_PROC_PAGE_MONITOR, not
      checked on the board kernel) and keep the ones of free colours for
      the hot state; or a CMA / dumb-buffer allocation (physically
      contiguous: its pages have consecutive colours) for the context's
      hot fields and tables;
    - compact the hot per-span state into one page, with its lines at
      offsets away from the stack's hot window measured at run time.

  prints "libGL: depth at colour + N in the page (S31GL_CCOLOR)" in C1
  (N ~2,032-2,048 for SHM / zero-copy segments).
  Prediction: -1.9% PSRAM traffic (-57 kB a frame); at tier 3's
  ~10 kB/ms that is ~0.6 ms of a ~115 ms frame, +0.5% fps - below what 4
  boots an arm can resolve, because the same model predicts a per-launch
  spread of sd ~9% of traffic from the page-colour lottery (section 3),
  i.e. a few % of fps between boots in EITHER arm. Expect the fps arms to
  read as a null; the decisive board check is the D-side refill counter
  (scripts/board/cachecnt-quake.sh, DBUS nxtlvl_rd of the Quake hart) per
  frame, C0 vs C1, where the model says -2%, and the boot-to-boot spread
  of the same counter, where it says ~9% sd. If the counter spread is
  that large, section 3 is the next lever, not this one.
  Regression: nothing but the depth buffer's address changes; windowed
  glxgears (MIT-SHM, 2 segments) and Doom / xcalc as usual.
