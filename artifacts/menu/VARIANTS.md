# lvdesk menu launch variants (2026-09-27)

The owner asked for a menu that lets them test every app at 320x240, 640x480
and 800x480, fullscreen and windowed, and at a chosen sound rate wherever the
app itself takes one. Every entry below uses only the app's OWN command-line
switches, taken from its source in this tree. No source changes, relinks,
environment steering or new config files. No rate is ever set unless the app's
own switch asks for it.

The file is `buildroot-external/board/esp32-s31/overlay/etc/lvdesk/menu.conf`.
The card's `/etc/lvdesk/menu.conf` must be identical to it.

## Menu structure and limits

The menu is generated by `artifacts/menu/genmenu.py`: run
`python3 artifacts/menu/genmenu.py <out>`, and do not hand-edit its output.
Every app's submenu is laid out the same way:

- **Tuned: ...** comes first. This is the known-good line, for example
  QuakeSpasm with `-mixspeed 11025 -heapsize 12288 -zone 384` at 320x240
  fullscreen.
- **Fullscreen > size > rate**, then **Window > size > rate**. Inside each
  size, the tuned rate is the first row.
- Extras follow: no sound, and a timedemo.

Every leaf label spells out the size, the mode and the rate
("640x480 window, 22 kHz (heavy)"), so a row still makes sense in Recent and
in search. Those two show only the parent's name. No submenu has more than 12
rows plus Back, so none of them scrolls (a 30 px row leaves room for about
14).

Where an app's own default rate needs no switch, the leaf passes none. That
applies to TyrQuake X11 at 48 kHz, which is also why it sorts first.

The menu has **225** entries. lvdesk limits, from `lvdesk/lvdesk.c`
`appmenu_load`:

| limit | value | past it |
|---|---|---|
| `MENU_MAX` | 512 entries (was 96), submenu lines count | dropped, and logged as `lvdesk: menu: N entries past MENU_MAX 512 dropped` |
| label | 39 chars (about 33 show) | truncated and logged |
| command | 199 chars | truncated and logged |
| line | 299 chars | the tail is discarded and logged. It used to come back as an entry of its own |

The memory cost is small. `mitems` is bss at 248 bytes an entry, and only the
entries a load fills become resident, which is about 56 kB for this menu.
`menu_disp` is 37 kB of address space, and only the rows actually shown are
touched.

"(heavy)" marks one of two things: 640x480 or 800x480 native rendering, or a
rate that makes the sound cache bigger. Either one may page, thrash or fail
to fit in 15.4 MB. Those rows are labelled rather than left out.

Fullscreen sizes are those xshim's VidMode offers (`vm_modes[]` in
`lvdesk/xshim.c`): 800x480, 640x480, 640x400, 640x384, 512x384, 480x300,
400x240, 320x240 and 320x200. TyrQuake aborts on a fullscreen size that is not
in that list, and all of the sizes used here are in it. An 800x480 *window* is
larger than the space the desktop has for it once the title bar is added, and
it is kept on purpose as a test.

## What the entries do

- **prboom:**
  - Tuned: 320x240 fullscreen.
  - Fullscreen and Window, each at 320x240, 320x200, 640x480 (heavy) and
    800x480 (heavy).
  - 320x240 fullscreen with no sound.
  - Timedemo, 320x200 fullscreen.
- **Chocolate Doom:**
  - Tuned: the existing fullscreen 320x240 profile at 48 kHz
    (`-extraconfig /etc/lvdesk/chocolate-fullscreen.cfg`).
  - Window at 320x240, 320x200, 640x480 and 800x480. Sound follows the main
    config.
  - The existing 320x200 window with no sound.
  - There is no fullscreen at other sizes, because that would need a new
    config file.
- **OpenTyrian:** its two entries, with sound and without.
- **sdlquake:**
  - Tuned: 320x240 fullscreen, at 11 kHz. The rate is fixed.
  - Fullscreen and Window, each at 320x240, 640x480 and 800x480.
  - No sound (`-nosound`).
  - Timedemo.
- **TyrQuake X11:**
  - Tuned: 320x240 fullscreen at its own default of 48 kHz, with `-mem 20`.
  - Fullscreen and Window, each at 3 sizes, and each size at 48, 11, 22 and
    44 kHz.
  - No sound.
  - Timedemo.
  - A lower rate is lighter, because sounds are resampled to that rate when
    they are cached.
- **QuakeSpasm:**
  - Tuned: 320x240 fullscreen at 11 kHz.
  - Fullscreen and Window, each at 3 sizes, and each size at 11, 22, 44 and
    48 kHz (`-mixspeed`).
  - No sound.
  - The `td` timedemo.
  - The mixer rate costs CPU, not memory. Fullscreen 800x480 is the panel
    size, where the desktop's GL render scale applies, and it is marked
    "(panel)". 640x480 fullscreen and every window render natively, and those
    rows are marked heavy.
- **TyrQuake GL:**
  - The same layout at 11, 22, 44 (heavy) and 48 (heavy) kHz via
    `-sndspeed`.
  - 44 and 48 kHz are heavy because sounds are cached at up to 4x the size
    inside the tuned 14336 KB heap.
- **GL demos:**
  - Gears (glxgears): tuned 300x300 window, panel-size fullscreen, and
    windows at 320x240, 640x480 (heavy) and 800x480 (heavy).
  - GLUT demos: 12 demos, each at its default size plus the other sizes via
    freeglut's `-geometry`. The demos call `glutInitWindowSize` before
    `glutInit`, so the switch takes effect.
  - Many windows: 2, 3 or 4 windows. The old entry passed no count, so it
    only printed its usage and exited.
  - Hidden line.
  - SDL 1.2 GL test: a 640x480 window and a fullscreen entry.
- **Apps** and **System** are unchanged.
